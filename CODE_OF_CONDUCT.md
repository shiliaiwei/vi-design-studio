# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and high-performing engineering environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the VI Design Studio project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy engineering community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community and engineering quality
* Adhering strictly to repository conventions, including the 16 Design Directives and the Zero-Emoji Policy across all commits, issues, and discussions

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
* Inserting emojis, decorative Unicode symbols, or non-standard characters into the codebase, documentation, or commit messages
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional engineering setting

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, project discussion boards, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---
